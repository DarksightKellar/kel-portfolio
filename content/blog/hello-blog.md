I've been meaning to start a blog for a while. Not because the world needs another voice, but because writing things down forces clarity. If you can't explain it simply, you probably don't understand it well enough.

This is the first step — a space to document what I'm learning, building, and occasionally breaking. Expect notes on software, systems, and the messy process of making things that work. Nothing polished. Just honest.
